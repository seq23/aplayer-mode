Billing and subscriptions

  • Bought on iPhone: manage or cancel in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Bought on Android or the web: manage or cancel from the app's Settings screen, or email us.
  • New phone? Sign in with the same account and tap Restore purchases in Settings.
