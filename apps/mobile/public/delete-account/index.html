Delete your A Player Mode account

A Player Mode, by Spry Labs · Last updated 2026-11-19

You can delete your A Player Mode account and all of its data at any time. You do not need the phone app to do it.

Three ways to ask

  1. In the app (iPhone or Android): Settings → Privacy & AI → Export & Delete → Delete my account, then Delete everything.
  2. On the web, without the app: sign in at app.aplayermode.com with the same email, Apple or Google sign-in you use in the app, then tap Delete my account and Delete everything.
  3. By email: write to support@aplayermode.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We reply to confirm and delete the account within 30 days.

How long it takes

A request from the app or the web is carried out within 24 hours, and you are signed out straight away. An email request is carried out within 30 days. Deletion cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, first name, account ID and your Apple or Google sign-in link.
  • Everything you told APM: setup answers, goals, plans, Tracks, check-ins, diary entries, coaching conversations and reviews, including any health information.
  • Connected accounts: calendar events and email-derived items. Google access is revoked at Google; every stored connection token is destroyed.
  • Usage and analytics records tied to you, notification tokens and the link to your billing record.

What is kept

  • A deletion receipt: your former account ID (a random number, not your email) and the dates the deletion was asked for and done, so we can show it happened.
  • Copies in encrypted backups, which expire on our providers' backup schedules.
  • Purchase records held by Apple, Google or our card processor, under their own rules.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first: in Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions), in your iPhone's App Store settings, or for a card subscription in Settings → Plan → Manage subscription.

Want a copy first? The same Export & Delete screen makes a full export of your data on the spot.
