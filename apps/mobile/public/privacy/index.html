A Player Mode

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-21

This policy explains how Spry Labs ("we", "us") handles personal information in A Player Mode: the iPhone and Android apps, the web app, and the services behind them ("APM"). The short version: we collect what APM needs to plan your days, we never sell your data, AI providers may not train on it, and you can export or delete it in the app at any time.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address; the first name you give APM to greet you (or the first name Sign in with Apple shares, if you allow it), which you can change in Settings; the account ID we assign you; and the identifier from Sign in with Apple or Google if you use them. Sign-in by email uses a one-time code; we do not store passwords.
  • What you tell APM: your setup answers (goals, routines, values, boundaries, wake time, life areas), daily check-ins and close-outs, mood and energy levels you choose to record, diary entries, coaching conversations and weekly reviews. Some of this can be sensitive: health and fitness information (how you like to move, food habits, weight, the daily energy score) and, if you answer it, whether pregnancy, diabetes medication, a heart condition or a history of disordered eating applies to you. We use it only to provide APM to you. Health information is collected only with your separate consent; our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy lists exactly what it covers and how to withdraw.
  • Connected accounts (only if you connect them):
    • Google: read-only access to your calendar events (calendar.events.readonly) and, only if you tap "Allow writes on this account" in Settings → Privacy → Connections, creating and changing events (calendar.events). APM does not ask for access to your calendar settings or to Gmail.
    • Microsoft: Calendars.Read/Calendars.ReadWrite, Mail.Read/Mail.ReadWrite/Mail.Send, and offline_access to stay connected.
    • Your phone's calendar, only if you connect it in Settings → Privacy → Connections and allow calendar access. Your phone gives an app access to all of its calendars at once (on iPhone this can include iCloud, Google, Outlook/Exchange and other accounts you have added to the phone). What APM then reads depends on the app version you have:
      • Version 1.0.1 and later: you tick which calendars APM may read, and APM reads only those. Nothing is ticked until you choose, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy → Connections. The names of your calendars and accounts stay on your phone.
      • Version 1.0: APM reads every calendar on your phone that it can see; you cannot pick some calendars and leave others out. This version also sends each calendar's name and the account it belongs to; our server discards those and does not store them.
      For events from 14 days ago to 90 days ahead in the calendars it reads, APM sends to our server each event's title, location, start and end time, time zone, whether it is all-day, whether it shows you as busy or free, its repeat rule and the organizer's name, plus an ID for the event and its calendar. It does not send event notes or the guest list. We store the events so APM can plan your day around your busy times and notice conflicts, and the relevant ones can be used by AI features as described in section 3. Each new sync replaces the stored events for that 14-day-back to 90-day-ahead window. When you untick a calendar (version 1.0.1 and later), its events are deleted from our server. To have all stored phone-calendar events deleted without deleting your account, tap "Remove phone calendar events" in Settings → Privacy → Connections (version 1.0.1 and later), or email support@aplayermode.com. To stop future reads, turn off calendar access for A Player Mode in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars (or Settings → Apps → A Player Mode → Calendars), or in Android's app permissions; this does not remove events already stored.
    From Google, APM reads only your calendar events, the minimum needed to find schedule conflicts and plan your day. From Microsoft, APM reads your calendar events and the content of the emails it checks (and can draft or send email only as you allow), the minimum needed to find schedule conflicts, deadlines and tasks, and it keeps derived facts (for example "meeting 2–3 pm Tuesday") rather than whole messages where it can. The access and refresh tokens for these accounts are encrypted at rest.
  • Subscription status and purchase history: your plan, where you bought it (card checkout, App Store or Google Play), renewal and expiry dates, from RevenueCat. We never receive your card or bank details: card payments are handled by RevenueCat and its payment processor Stripe, and store payments by Apple and Google.
  • Device and usage data: a push-notification token if you allow notifications, your time zone, app version, and product events, which record how you interact with APM (which screens and steps were used, and timings). Product events never contain the text you write.
  • Security logs: an audit record of sign-ins, permission changes and every action APM takes on your behalf, so you can see them in Settings → Privacy → Activity.

2. How we use it

  • To provide APM: build your plan, print each day's agenda, run check-ins, weekly reviews and coaching, send the reminders you turn on, and take the actions you approve or allow under Autopilot.
  • To keep your plan in step with your subscription.
  • To keep APM secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To improve APM using aggregated, de-identified product events. We do not use your private content (answers, diary, email, calendar, coaching messages) to train AI models, and we do not allow our AI providers to.
  • To contact you about your account, security, and changes to these policies.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and APM shows no ads.

3. AI processing

Some features send the relevant part of your data to an AI model to produce a plan, a coaching reply or a draft. These requests go through OpenRouter to model providers we have approved. For private data we require providers that do not train on it and do not retain it (OpenRouter's "data collection: deny" and zero-data-retention settings), and we send only what the task needs. Several features (for example, the built-in coaching steps) run without any AI model; the app says so. Settings → Privacy → How AI works shows which features use AI.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that process it for us under contract, only to run APM:

  • Supabase (database and sign-in), with your data stored in the United States.
  • Cloudflare (the APM API and web app hosting).
  • OpenRouter and the approved AI model providers it routes to (see section 3).
  • RevenueCat and Stripe (card checkout and subscriptions), and Apple and Google (store purchases).
  • Google and Microsoft, only for the accounts you connect, as you allow: Google for the calendars you connect, Microsoft for the calendars and mail you connect.
  • Expo, Apple and Google push services, to deliver notifications you turn on.
  • Resend, to send account emails such as the yearly renewal reminder (your email address and plan details).

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a merger or sale of the business (in which case this policy continues to apply to your data).

5. Google user data

APM's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the calendar features you see in APM, is never used for advertising, is never sold, and is never used to train AI models. People at Spry Labs do not read it unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your plan, goals, history and diary: for as long as you have an account, until you change or delete them.
  • Connected-account tokens: until you disconnect the account or delete your APM account.
  • Raw email content: only as long as needed to derive the facts APM uses. Calendar events: kept as described in section 1 (each sync replaces the stored window; a disconnected Google or Microsoft account's events are deleted when you disconnect it).
  • Security and action audit records: as long as needed for security and legal purposes.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your data from our active systems; residual copies in encrypted backups expire on the providers' backup schedules.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Export: download a copy of your data any time in Settings → Privacy → Export & Delete.
  • Delete: delete your account and all of its data in Settings → Privacy → Export & Delete. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it first in Settings → Plan → Manage subscription (card) or in your App Store or Google Play settings (store).
  • Correct: edit your answers, goals and settings in the app.
  • Disconnect: remove a Google or Microsoft connection in Settings → Privacy → Connections, or from your Google or Microsoft account. For your phone's calendar, untick calendars or tap "Remove phone calendar events" in Settings → Privacy → Connections (version 1.0.1 and later; on version 1.0, email us), and turn off calendar access for A Player Mode in your phone's settings to stop future reads (see section 1).
  • Notifications: turn them off in Settings or in your phone's settings.
  • Health data consent: give or withdraw it in Settings → Privacy → Consumer health data (see the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy).

Depending on where you live (for example, California or the EU/UK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port or restrict use of your personal information, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Use the in-app controls above or email support@aplayermode.com; we will not treat you differently for exercising them. If you are outside the United States, your data is transferred to and processed in the United States.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, connected-account credentials are encrypted at rest, database access is limited per user by row-level security, and every action APM takes for you is logged. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will notify you as the law requires.

9. The website aplayermode.com

The marketing site aplayermode.com is separate from the app and collects much less:

  • Session analytics. The site uses Microsoft Clarity only on a page with a sign-up form (today, the launch-updates form on the home page), to see how that page is used (clicks, scrolling, page views) so we can fix confusing steps. Clarity does not run on the site's other pages, it is set to mask what you type into the form, and it is not used in the app or the web app at app.aplayermode.com.
  • Launch updates. If you tick the box and give your email to get launch updates, we keep that email and the date you agreed, only to send those updates. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Pinterest visits. We count visits that arrive from our Pinterest pins by the campaign tag in the link only; no IP address, device or cookie is kept for this.
  • Hosting and checkout. Cloudflare hosts the site. The Billionaire High Performance Coach digital product is sold on Gumroad, under Gumroad's own privacy policy.

10. Children

APM is for adults 18 and over, and asks you to confirm you are 18 or older before you can use an account; we keep a dated record of that confirmation. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We will update the "Last updated" date when this policy changes and tell you in the app or by email before a material change takes effect.
