# **PRIVACY & DATA SAFETY**

*READ BEFORE INSTALLATION*

This system works best when you can speak honestly.

Large language models are not people — but they are also not private journals by default. You should decide your privacy level before setup.

## **Recommended Privacy Setup (Optional but Encouraged)**

You may choose to create a separate account used only for this system. Many users prefer to:  
• Use a new email address  
• Avoid linking work or personal identities  
• Keep this system separate from daily browsing

This is not required. It is simply a comfort and boundary option.

## **Platform Settings**

Most LLM platforms allow you to control whether conversations are used to improve the model. If you prefer additional privacy:  
• Turn off conversation history sharing (if available)  
• Turn off model training / data usage in account settings  
• Avoid uploading sensitive documents or identifying files

Settings vary by platform and may change over time. Review your platform’s privacy settings before starting.

## **What to Share**

You never need to share:  
• Legal names  
• Addresses  
• Account numbers  
• Passwords  
• Confidential business information

Use descriptions instead of identifiers.

Example:  
Instead of "my employer is\[Company Name\]," write "my job."  
Instead of names, use roles ("a friend," "a client," "a partner").

*Important Note:*  
*This system works on patterns and decisions — not identity. You can receive full benefit without revealing personal identifying information.*
